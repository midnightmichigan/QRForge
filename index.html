Why static? Why offline?

Static codes don't expire

Many “free” generators give you a dynamic code: it points at their server, which redirects to your link. If you stop paying — or they shut down — your printed codes die. QRForge writes your text directly into the code. There is no redirect, no account, and nothing to expire.

Private by construction

Wi-Fi passwords, contact cards, and internal links are sensitive. This page has no server, no analytics, and no network calls — open it from a file or airplane mode and it still works. Your data literally cannot leave your device.

Print-ready SVG

Download a crisp vector SVG for flyers, menus, and signage — it scales to any size without blurring. PNG export is included for quick digital use.
